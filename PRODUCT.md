# Product

## Register

product

## Users

LCA tenant administrators and operations staff use this internal application during day-to-day catalogue and order-management work. Their immediate job is to maintain accurate product, category, pricing, and inventory data quickly and safely within their own tenant.

## Product Purpose

Modernize verified LCA business capabilities into a shared-schema, tenant-aware SaaS platform. Success means operational users can complete migrated administration workflows in the new application with trustworthy data, clear validation, and strict tenant isolation while legacy ownership is retired capability by capability.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and workmanlike. The interface should feel calm under repetitive operational use, favor precise status communication, and avoid decorative novelty that competes with the data.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a consumer storefront, a promotional SaaS landing page, or an analytics dashboard filled with decorative metrics. Avoid glass effects, excessive cards, oversized marketing typography, ornamental motion, and unfamiliar custom controls.

## Design Principles

- Put the current operational task and its data first.
- Make destructive, irreversible, and bulk actions explicit before execution.
- Keep tenant and authentication state implicit and trusted rather than user-editable.
- Explain empty, loading, validation, and conflict states in actionable language.
- Prefer familiar controls and consistent density over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA fundamentals: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, semantic labels, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, clear errors, and reduced-motion support. Tables and forms must remain usable on narrow screens without hiding essential actions.
